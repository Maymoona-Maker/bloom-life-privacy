Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to Habit Bloom. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information our application collects, how it is used, and your rights while using Habit Bloom.

Information We Collect

Habit Bloom does not require you to create an account.

The application does not ask for your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Location
  • Photos
  • Microphone access
  • Camera access

The application stores your habit progress, garden progress, coins, achievements, settings, and other gameplay information locally on your own device.

Local Storage

All application data remains on your device. Bloom Studio cannot access, view, or collect your personal progress or habit information. If you uninstall the application, your locally stored data may be permanently removed.

Advertising

Habit Bloom uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device identifiers, advertising identifiers, and usage information according to Google's own Privacy Policy.

For more information please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Habit Bloom is suitable for users of all ages. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Any future changes will be published on this page.
